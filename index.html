Or right-click any text file in Google Drive and choose “Open with QuietPad.”

How it works

  1. Sign in with your Google account.
  2. Pick a text file from your Drive, or start a new one.
  3. Edit. Changes save straight back to that same file — nothing is copied, converted, or stored anywhere but your own Drive.
