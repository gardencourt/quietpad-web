Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

What QuietPad is

QuietPad is a plain-text and Markdown note editor, available as an Android app and as this browser-based companion editor. Neither has a server of its own: both talk directly to Google Drive's own API, using your own Google account, to read and save your files. Signing in with Google is optional in the Android app — skip it, and your notes stay on that device only, never touching Drive or any other service.

What we access

QuietPad requests two narrow Google Drive scopes, and nothing broader:

  • drive.file — access only to files you explicitly open through QuietPad: a file you create in the app, one you pick via its in-app file picker, or one you open with “Open with QuietPad” directly from Google Drive. QuietPad cannot see, list, or access any other file in your Drive.
  • drive.appdata — a hidden, per-app data folder, invisible in your ordinary Drive view, used only by the Android app to record whether this Google account has already used QuietPad's free trial before. It holds a trial start date and nothing else — no note content, no file names, nothing you've written.

What we store

Nothing on a QuietPad server of our own — we don't operate one. In the Android app, your notes, diary entries, and attachments are stored as plain files on your own device, indexed in a local database the app keeps for itself, and optionally synced to your own Google Drive if you've signed in. In this browser editor, a file's content exists only in your own Google Drive and, temporarily, your browser tab's memory while it's open.

Other on-device features

The Android app's optional App Lock uses your device's own fingerprint/face unlock (Android's BiometricPrompt) to lock the app itself — QuietPad never receives, stores, or sees any biometric data; that stays entirely between you and your device's operating system. Reminders are ordinary local notifications scheduled on your device and are never transmitted anywhere. The app's one-time unlock purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing — QuietPad never sees your payment details.

Third parties

QuietPad does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts of any kind, and does not share any data with third parties. The only other parties involved are Google's own Drive, Sign-In, and Play Billing services, each governed by Google's own privacy policy.
