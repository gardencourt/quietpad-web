The short version

QuietPad is offered as-is, with no warranty. New installs get a 14-day free trial of full use. After the trial, your existing notes stay readable and editable, but creating new notes needs a one-time unlock, bought through Google Play at the price shown there for your country — there's no subscription. Refunds are handled under Google Play's own policies, not ours.

QuietPad edits files that live on your own device and, if you sign in, your own Google Drive — we never take a copy or a backup of anything you write. If something goes wrong with a save, your file is still exactly what your device or Google Drive itself says it is; we have no separate record to fall back on.
